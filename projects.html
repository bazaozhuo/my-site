Shipped

  • 01

    Deutsch-Lernzimmer

    Live

    Web · German · study tool

    An open study site for German learners between A2 and B1: 703 words, a searchable knowledge index, a word challenge and a custom 404 page. Bilingual, light and dark themes, no backend.

    Core 703 words kept in eight data files, one per topic group. The vocabulary table, the challenge and the daily five all read from the same source, so adding a word is a one-file change.

Planned

  • 02

    Orbit

    Planned · 3–4 days

    Astronomy · canvas · maths

    Drag a time slider and see where the planets actually were on that date — “where was Mars the day I was born?”. The state lives in the URL, so any date is shareable.

    Core Real ephemeris, not a decorative animation: Keplerian orbital elements plus a Newton iteration to solve Kepler's equation. The same page gives a different answer in ten years, because the sky does.

  • 03

    Silk & Bars

    Planned · 4–5 days

    Music · Chinese samples · English rap · Web Audio

    A browser sampler for putting traditional Chinese instruments under an English rap verse. Load a short phrase of guzheng, erhu, pipa or dizi, cut it onto a 16-step grid, layer a drum pattern, then record your own voice over the top and export the mix as one file.

    Core A real audio clock, not setInterval alone: a lookahead scheduler reads AudioContext.currentTime and queues each slice a fraction of a second early, so the grid does not drift after two minutes. The finished mix is rendered through OfflineAudioContext and encoded to WAV in the page — no server, no library.

    Every sample gets a credit line; only self-recorded or public-domain recordings go in.

  • 04

    Time Capsule

    Planned · 2–3 days

    Future · cryptography · no backend

    Write a letter to your future self, lock it with a password and an unlock date. Before that date nobody can open it — not even the site, because the site never sees the key.

    Core Real encryption in the browser: PBKDF2 derives a key from the passphrase, AES-GCM encrypts the letter, only the ciphertext is stored. Forget the passphrase and it is gone — that is the design, and the page says so.

  • 05

    Void Terminal

    Planned · 3–4 days

    Science fiction · parser · narrative

    A retro terminal aboard a ship that stopped answering. You only have the keyboard: scan the deck, read the black box, decrypt what is left, open the right bay. The ending is a sentence you assemble.

    Core A small command parser with a state machine — tokenise the line, resolve it against the current world state, print the result with a typing delay. Not a pile of if-statements; commands unlock as the story moves.

Five entries: one shipped, four planned. Status is honest — the planned ones are not pretending to be finished.
